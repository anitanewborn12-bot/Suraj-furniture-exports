Who We Are

Building Trust Through Furniture

Established in 2012, Suraj Furniture Exports is a family-owned furniture manufacturing and export business founded by Suraj Chauhan

What began as a vision to deliver quality furniture has grown into a business built on craftsmanship, reliability and long-term relationships

Since 2020, the next generation has continued to lead the business strengthening operations, expanding product offerings and developing opportunities in domestic and international markets
