About Our Company

Who We Are

Building Trust Through Furniture

Suraj Furniture Exports is a family-established furniture business focused on manufacturing and supplying furniture for residential and commercial spaces.

Our journey is built on the values of quality, practical design, customer relationships and dependable service. We work with clients to understand their requirements and provide furniture solutions suited to their projects.

Our Journey

A Family Business with a Vision

Founded by Suraj Chauhan, the business was established with the aim of building a trusted name in the furniture industry.

Today, the next generation continues this journey with a focus on strengthening operations, developing customer relationships and exploring opportunities in domestic and international markets.

What We Do

Furniture Solutions for Different Spaces

Our range includes dining tables, chairs, beds, cabinets, sideboards, coffee tables, consoles and customised furniture. We serve residential requirements as well as commercial and wholesale projects.

We welcome enquiries from businesses, architects, interior professionals, project buyers and furniture wholesalers.
